OUR SERVICES

Personalized Dental Services for Every Stage of Life

From routine preventive checkups to advanced smile restoration, our experienced team provides thorough and gentle treatments under one roof.

1. Routine Cleaning & Exams

Thorough plaque removal, gentle ultrasonic scaling, digital cavity screening, and proactive periodontal evaluations to keep your teeth and gums vibrant.

Schedule exam →

2. Professional Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening and tailored take-home trays formulated with enamel-safe brightening agents to lift stubborn discoloration with minimal tooth sensitivity.

Brighten your smile →

3. Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacements. Using precise 3D cone-beam scans, we place durable titanium posts and custom porcelain crowns.

Explore implants →

4. Invisalign Clear Aligners

Straighten misaligned teeth discreetly without metal brackets or wires. Enjoy removable trays that let you eat, brush, and floss with complete ease.

Consult on aligners →

5. Emergency Dental Care

Fast, attentive relief for chipped teeth, severe toothaches, lost fillings, and oral trauma. We reserve dedicated daily slots for immediate acute care.

Get urgent relief →

6. Pediatric & Kids Dentistry

Gentle, stress-free appointments designed to build lifelong healthy oral habits for toddlers, children, and teens with positive, fun reinforcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scheduling, dental insurance, treatment expectations, and preparing for your appointment.

Most patients benefit from visiting every six months for a professional cleaning and examination. Patients with a history of periodontal disease, frequent cavities, or specific systemic medical conditions may be advised to visit every three to four months for closer monitoring.

If you have a knocked-out tooth, rinse it gently with milk or saline (avoid scrubbing the root) and attempt to reinsert it into the socket, or store it in a container of cold milk. Call our office immediately at (512) 555-0199. For uncontrolled bleeding or suspected jaw fractures, visit the nearest emergency room.

Yes, we accept and file claims with most major PPO dental plans. Our front office staff will verify your benefits in advance and provide an itemized breakdown of expected insurance contribution versus estimated patient co-pays before any treatment begins.

Our in-office whitening procedure takes approximately 60 to 90 minutes from start to finish. For patients selecting custom take-home whitening trays, daily applications usually produce noticeable shade improvements within 7 to 14 days.

In alignment with the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, we recommend bringing your child in when their first tooth erupts or by their first birthday. Early visits focus on quick, friendly examinations and educating parents on decay prevention.

Please bring your government ID, dental insurance card (if applicable), and an updated list of current medications. Arriving 10 minutes early allows you to complete our brief digital intake form. If you have recent dental X-rays from a prior clinic, you can request them to be emailed to us prior to your arrival.
